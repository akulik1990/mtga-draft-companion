Each point is one pick of the draft. A conditional logit over top drafters' (60%+ over 50+ games) actual choices finds how much each input moves the odds of taking a card at that pick, with the other inputs held fixed. Units: change in log-odds per one standard deviation of the input within that pick's packs, so inputs are comparable. Bands are ±1 SE. Above zero means top drafters lean toward cards high on it.
