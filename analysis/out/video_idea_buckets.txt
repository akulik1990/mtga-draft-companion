### Fixing / dual land over a marginal playable (when the pack has nothing, playables are covered, or a splash is likely) (18 videos)
  - [mqZ-16] A dual land for the splash colour is worth more than an off-colour playable once the pool holds splashable bombs; fixing value rises with the number of splash cards already taken.
  - [CWs-12] A land that fixes a possible splash is worth taking over a mediocre playable early (white-black dual over Inspired Tethermage).
  - [994-11] Take the flexible fixing land (Room of Refuge) over playables when the pack has nothing for the deck, because the deck is casting big things and may splash.
  - [re7-11] Dual lands are taken when nothing else is playable, but one with no use is a late pick (Innovative Commons): the blue-red land is fine, 'sure, why not'.
