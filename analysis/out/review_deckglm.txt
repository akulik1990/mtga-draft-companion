## 3. Weighting: games-weighted (as run) vs one deck one vote

Deck games are 3 to 9 under the 7-win / 3-loss stop. Weighting by games gives winning decks more weight; the binomial likelihood is valid
under the stopping rule but the clustered SE and any misspecification are not. Unweighted = GLM on the deck win share with weight 1.

| sample | weighted | unweighted |
|---|---|---|
| primary | +0.19 [-0.11, +0.48] | +0.33 [-0.11, +0.77] |
| all skills | +0.25 [+0.10, +0.40] | +0.37 [+0.15, +0.60] |
